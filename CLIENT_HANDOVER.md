# Client handover checklist

- Identify owners of WordPress, hosting, domain/DNS, email, backups, SMTP and the repository; use individual accounts and MFA where supported.
- Supply approved privacy/terms content, business hours/address if public, notification recipients, retention and deletion decisions, and product/image approvals.
- Agree who checks enquiries, what happens when notification delivery fails, who can access attachments and how pending submissions are reviewed.
- Provide the client with tested backup/restore instructions, support contact, update schedule, domain renewal and recovery procedures after staging verification.

The current implementation is not approved for launch.
